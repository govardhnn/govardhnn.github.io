Digital Design

A five-part series, in reading order. Dally & Harting and Harris & Harris are the backbone; Weste & Harris supplies the circuit-level grounding.

  1. Boolean Algebra: axioms, canonical forms, minimization, number systems and codes
  2. Combinational Circuits: gates, muxes, decoders, adders and prefix trees, shifters, hazards
  3. Sequential Circuits: latches, flip-flops, registers, counters, memory, FSMs
  4. RTL Design Guide: SystemVerilog discipline, always blocks, blocking vs nonblocking, testbenches
  5. Synthesis, Timing and Power: synthesis flow, STA, logical effort, CDC, reset, power techniques

Where the hardware goes next: these blocks assemble into the machines of the Computer Architecture series, starting with Simple Instruction Pipeline and its history.
